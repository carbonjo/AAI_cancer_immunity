# Starter reading

Short list for David Graham and Dominic Sciarrino. This is literacy, not a comprehensive review. Add papers the SME assigns; do not pad it with unvetted chatbot citations.

## Information flow in the cell

- Crick F. Central dogma of molecular biology. *Nature*. 1970;227:561–563.
- Alberts B, et al. *Molecular Biology of the Cell* — chapters on transcription, gene regulation, and signaling (any recent edition). Use as a textbook spine, not as a citation dump.

## Single-cell RNA sequencing

- Zheng GXY, et al. Massively parallel digital transcriptional profiling of single cells. *Nat Commun*. 2017;8:14049. (droplet / 10x-style conceptual workflow)
- Luecken MD, Theis FJ. Current best practices in single-cell RNA-seq analysis: a tutorial. *Mol Syst Biol*. 2019;15:e8746. (QC through interpretation, including what not to overclaim)

## Cancer cell biology

- Hanahan D, Weinberg RA. Hallmarks of cancer: the next generation. *Cell*. 2011;144:646–674.
- Hanahan D. Hallmarks of cancer: new dimensions. *Cancer Discov*. 2022;12:31–46. (optional extension; tutorial core stays on 2011)

## Cancer immunity

- Chen DS, Mellman I. Oncology meets immunology: the cancer-immunity cycle. *Immunity*. 2013;39:1–10.
- Sharma P, Allison JP. The future of immune checkpoint therapy. *Science*. 2015;348:56–61.

## How to use this list

Read for mechanism. When a figure in a paper disagrees with tutorial wording, **do not silently “fix” the tutorial** — write a `needs-SME-review` note and ask Dr. Krolewski. Methods questions go to Dr. Carbonara.
