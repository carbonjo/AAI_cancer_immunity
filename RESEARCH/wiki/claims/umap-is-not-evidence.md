Teaching claim from the learning-goals source (module 2). GeoTyper §IV-C uses UMAP to *visualize* Seurat clusters in a lower-dimensional space and to compare label methods on the same plot (their Fig. 2). They also mention t-SNE as a similar view. That use is display, not a spatial or lineage measurement.
