> A cell’s state is an information-processing program. scRNA-seq reads a noisy projection of that program. Cancer corrupts the program and its niche. Immunity is the recognition system that can clear, ignore, or be blocked by that niche — and single-cell data is one way to look at both sides at once.

Student outcomes in that source: (1) information flow beyond slogan-level dogma; (2) droplet scRNA-seq through the count matrix plus three failure modes; (3) hallmarks as a map and drivers vs states; (4) cancer-immunity cycle, PD-1/CTLA-4, what hot/cold TIME does not mean; (5) check the AI tutor with the SME.

Non-goals: not a medical course; not a Scanpy/Seurat lab; not a complete immunology textbook; not [Dr. Krolewski](../people/john-krolewski.md)’s unpublished results. Path: about 25–35 minutes per module including quiz; tutor optional.
