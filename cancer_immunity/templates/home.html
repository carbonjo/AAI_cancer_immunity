Four modules, one argument

Information flow, single-cell RNA, cancer biology, and cancer immunity

A cell’s state is an information-processing program. Single-cell RNA sequencing reads a noisy projection of that program. Cancer corrupts the program and its niche. Immunity is the recognition system that can clear, ignore, or be blocked by that niche.

How to use this tutorial

  1. Read a module in order. Use the interactive figure until you can explain it out loud.
  2. Take the checkpoint quiz. Answers are scored on the server; you do not need an API key.
  3. Ask the AI tutor to rephrase a mechanism. Treat its replies as study aids, not as sign-off from {{ team.sme }}.
  4. Carry questions into research notes in this repository. Disease-specific claims wait for the SME; app and methods questions go to {{ team.advisor }}.
